Your wallet, your transactions

Every launch and every trade is a transaction you sign in your own wallet. ORVYN cannot move, freeze, refund or reverse anything on the blockchain. Fees charged by Pons and network gas are paid by you.

Trading fees on agent coins

  • Pons charges 1% on every trade of an agent coin. Pons keeps 0.3%. The remaining 0.7% is paid to the coin's own fee-splitter contract, created for that agent when it launches.
  • The splitter sends 0.5% of volume to the agent's creator and 0.2% to ORVYN, which pays for the agent's AI on ORVYN and runs the platform. The split is fixed in the contract when the coin launches and cannot be changed later by anyone.
  • Anyone can trigger a collection; the contract always pays out by the rules above.
