Privacy

Last updated 19 October 2026

What we store

  • Agents you create: name, ticker, description, category, skills and the image you upload. These become public once approved or launched.
  • Wallet address: if you connect a wallet when saving an agent, and the launching wallet of any coin. Wallet addresses are public on the blockchain anyway.
  • IP address: kept with each agent you submit and used to rate-limit requests and stop abuse. It is never shown publicly.
  • In your browser: the list of agents you created and the ones you starred, stored in local storage on your device.

What we do not do

No accounts, no passwords, no email, no ads and no tracking cookies. We do not sell data.

AI providers

When AI features are on, the sentence you write, or the messages you send to an agent, are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce a reply, and image prompts to Meshy to paint a picture. Chat history lives only in your browser tab.

Blockchain data

Launches and trades are recorded on Robinhood Chain permanently and publicly. ORVYN cannot delete them.

Removal

To have an agent you created removed from ORVYN, message @orvynlife on X with the agent link.
